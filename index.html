What we do

Everything below happens at your yard, job site, ranch or roadside. Nothing needs to be hauled in first.

Heavy equipment repair

Field diagnosis and repair on excavators, loaders, backhoes, dozers and skid steers.

  • Hydraulics
  • Engines and cooling
  • Brakes and final drives
  • Electrical
  • Undercarriage

Tractor and semi truck

Tractors, implements, trailers and over-the-road trucks kept running through the season.

  • Air systems
  • Clutches and drivelines
  • PTO and hitches
  • Preventive service

Welding

Structural and repair welding on site, in stainless, aluminum and steel.

  • MIG
  • TIG
  • Stick
  • Cracked frames and buckets
  • Broken castings

Fabrication

One-off parts built to fit when the replacement is discontinued, back-ordered or never existed.

  • Brackets and mounts
  • Guards and skid plates
  • Racks and toolboxes
  • Trailer work
  • Custom hitches

Sandblasting

Rust, scale and old paint taken back to clean metal, ready for primer the same day.

  • Frames and trailers
  • Buckets and implements
  • Tanks
  • Surface prep for coating

Mobile tire service and sales

Tires changed, repaired and supplied where the machine is parked.

  • Equipment and ag tires
  • Semi and trailer
  • Flat repair
  • New tire sales
